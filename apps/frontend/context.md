# Frontend Context: Salora

Salora is an all-in-one online booking system designed specifically for salons and studios. The platform helps salon owners and teams manage appointments, reduce no-shows, and automate daily operations—so they can focus on their clients and their craft, not on admin hassle.

## What does Salora do?

- **Hassle-free online bookings:** Clients can book their own appointments directly in your agenda, 24/7, from any device. No more endless inboxes or double bookings.
- **No-show prevention:** Built-in anti no-show technology helps reduce missed appointments by up to 90%.
- **All-in-one dashboard:** See all your appointments, payments, and client info in one clear overview.
- **Online payments:** Let clients pay online (including deposits), so you don’t have to handle payments at the counter.
- **Client self-service portal:** Clients can manage their own appointments, reducing your email load and giving you more control.
- **Employee scheduling:** Easily manage staff availability, holidays, and working hours.
- **Automated setup & support:** Salora handles the implementation from A to Z, so you can get started without stress.
- **Integrations:** Works with WordPress, Shopify, Wix, and more for seamless website integration.
- **Review system:** Collect and manage client reviews.
- **Scalable plans:** From solo salons to growing studios with unlimited staff, Salora adapts to your needs.

## Why salons love Salora

- Prevents double bookings and no-shows
- Saves time by automating repetitive tasks
- Gives clients a modern, easy booking experience
- Everything is managed in one place—no more switching between tools

Salora is trusted by 500+ salons every day to keep their business running smoothly and their calendars full.

For more, visit [salora.app](https://salora.app/).
